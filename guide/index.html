FusionSpace

Everything I make, under one name. It is drawn from how rockets are shaped and how stars are classified. Four nose cones, each a real aerodynamic profile, lean together in one cluster, and a gradient runs across them from cool M-class orange to hot O-class blue.

The mark

The 2023 logo was a cluster of four stars. Rev C keeps the cluster and turns each star into a nose cone: Von Kármán for the main cone, conical and elliptical for the two wings (0.4 and 0.3 of the main cone's base radius) and a tangent ogive (0.5) for the tail. The four sit in a diamond: the ogive trails behind the main cone on the same axis, which keeps the cluster close to square. Every cone has a deep notch at its base, and the cluster is laid out on a grid that leans 45° toward the blue end, so it reads as a launch. One gradient sweeps across the whole cluster, so the west cone comes out warm and the east cone cool.

Real profiles

The four shapes are the standard nose-cone profiles from rocketry software, drawn from their standard equations with the same length-to-radius ratio. Edges are true lines, arcs and ellipses (Von Kármán is traced with smooth curves), and logo/mark/fusion-space-mark-50mm.dxf is drawn only with lines and arcs for FreeCAD, laser and CNC work.

One sweep

The gradient always runs left to right, from M orange to O blue, across the full width of the cluster.

Three modes

Use the gradient version wherever color prints. Use one-color Void or white for engraving, stamps, laser cutting and single-color vinyl.

SHEET 2 / 9

Lockups

The wordmark is FusionSpace, one word, set in Cascadia Mono SemiBold with the same gradient. It's outlined in every file.

Clear space

Keep 0.25 H clear on every side, where H is the height of the cone cluster.

Minimum size

Cluster: 24 px or 8 mm tall. Below 24 px, as in favicons, use the favicon files: they are the full cluster, hinted for 16 px (the two wing cones become small arrows). For cutting, use the DXF: its feet are at least 0.5 mm wide at 50 mm. For stock thicker than about 1 mm, cut the mark larger or ask the vendor. On light backgrounds, a horizontal lockup under 32 px tall uses the one-color Void lockup.

Proportions

Horizontal: cap height 0.522 H (the mark is 1.92× the caps), gap 0.35 × the cap height (0.183 H). Stacked: cap height 0.472 H, gap 0.10 H. Only these two arrangements are approved: mark before the wordmark, and mark above it.

Color

Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Fusion gradient

The brand's signature color. It runs from class M to class O, passing through rose and lavender so the middle doesn't turn gray. It is the same gradient on dark and light backgrounds: every part of it holds at least 3:1 against white and 6:1 against Void.

Spectral

The source palette, sampled from the Harvard classification's Vega-relative chromaticity. O and M are the gradient ends; B to K are the sampled star colors. Use it for charts and graphics, not for text on light backgrounds.

Core

Text on light

For links, labels and warnings on Paper or white. The gradient ends hold 3.0 : 1 on white: enough for graphics and large text, not for body text, so colored text on light uses Ion and Ember, which pass WCAG AA. On dark backgrounds, use the gradient ends. They are not logo colors: the logo uses the gradient, or the flat two-tone cones, on every background. In the tokens they are the Signal group.

Setup

  1. Install every font in type/fonts (double-click, then Install).
  2. Inkscape palette: copy color/fusion-space.gpl to ~/Library/Application Support/org.inkscape.Inkscape/config/inkscape/palettes/, then restart Inkscape and pick “FusionSpace” in the Swatches panel.
  3. FreeCAD: in TechDraw, choose Insert Page using Template and pick a sheet from templates/freecad. The title-block fields show up under the page's Editable Texts. The sheets are transparent, so the page color shows through; if your TechDraw page color is dark, use the -dark sheets (light lines), and print or export PDFs from the light ones.
  4. Each logo file has a hidden, locked “live text” layer. To edit the wordmark, unhide that layer. Otherwise leave the outlined version as it is.

Kit

Ready-made files for every place the brand shows up. All of them are generated from the mark, so they never drift.

Five color modes cover every background and process. Use the gradient wherever color reproduces well. Use flat two-tone where only spot colors work (screen print, vinyl, embroidery): each cone takes the nearer end of the gradient, so the west and south cones are warm and the main and east cones are cool. Use one color for engraving, stamps and single-ink print.

Light and dark

Every banner, avatar, cover and slide comes in a dark (Void) and a light (Paper) version. On GitHub, the README snippet swaps them with the reader's theme.

Two-tone colors

The same cones on every background: M orange #DA7C30 and O blue #768DF5. Only the wordmark changes, white on dark and Void on light. Ion and Ember stay text and UI colors.

SHEET 9 / 9

Products

How FusionSpace tools, apps, devices and rockets are designed. The rules are in product/.

The brand says who made it; the product system says how the thing behaves. FusionSpace products are drawn, not decorated: every screen is a sheet from a drawing set, with line types that mean what they mean on a drawing, a title block, and a designation like a part. They show their working, say how far to trust each number, and are built for a launch field.

1 · Drawn, not decorated

Every screen is a sheet from a drawing set: line types, title blocks, balloons. No ornament.

2 · Show the working

Formula, constants, inputs, sources and dates one step from every result.

3 · Say how far to trust it

Measured, predicted or copied, with its spread. Never a go/no-go verdict.

4 · Built for the field

Offline, readable in sun, usable with gloves, printable in black.

5 · Quiet until it matters

Four reserved signal colors, always with a word and a shape. Unused is gray, never red.

6 · One sweep

The gradient once per view, as identity: never a button, a status or a data scale.

7 · Numbered like parts

Every product has a designation and a revision, on screen and on the part.

8 · Native where it counts

The platform owns behavior; FusionSpace owns content.

Signal colors

Four colors that mean a state, kept apart from the brand colors and used with a word and a shape: Flare for danger, Sodium for caution, Aurora for normal, Nebula for predicted. They follow the flight-deck and spacecraft standards (14 CFR 25.1322, FAA AC 25-11B, NASA-STD-3001).
