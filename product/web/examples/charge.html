Show the maths
V     = π/4 × D² × L = π/4 × (3.90 in)² × 12.0 in = 143.4 in³
grams = 0.00052 × P × V
      = 0.00052 × 15 psi × 143.4 in³
      = 1.118 g

0.00052 g per psi per in³ is the ideal-gas yield of black powder at 3307 °R (R = 22.16 ft·lbf/(lbm·°R)), with no losses: the same as the common rule of 0.006 g × D² × L at 15 psi.

How far to trust it

An ideal-gas estimate. Real bays leak and real powder varies, so the charge that separates on the ground is the one to fly. In this example log, ground tests needed 0.9 to 1.3 times the estimate.

Caution

Ground-test before every first flight, and after any change to the bay, pins or powder.
